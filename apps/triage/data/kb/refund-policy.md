title: Refund policy
---
You can ask for a refund within 30 days of delivery. The order must be delivered and not refunded before.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval.
Refunds above 500 USD are reviewed by a support lead before approval.
Orders that are not delivered yet cannot be refunded; cancel the order instead.
